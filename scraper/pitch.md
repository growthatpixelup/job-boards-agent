# Pixelup Labs — what application briefs may say

Source: "Pixelup Labs — Positioning & ICP Engine" (July 2026). `brief.ts` sends this file to Claude with every
job it writes an application brief for. Only facts written here may appear in a brief, so keep it true and
current: edit it to add a case study, change availability or retire a claim. A change here rewrites the
briefs of every open lead on the next scrape.

## Who we are

- Pixelup Labs is the enterprise-readiness design partner for funded B2B startups, most of them AI-native.
- We rebuild a startup's brand, website and product design around the opinion the founder holds about their
  market, so enterprise buyers stop hesitating.
- What we do: brand identity and strategy, positioning and messaging, marketing sites and landing pages
  (Webflow, Framer or custom Next.js), product UI/UX, design systems, pitch and sales decks.
- Small senior team. Founder-led creative direction on every project. Bangalore-based, SF-calibrated.
- Speed: 2-week brand sprints, ~3-week sites, ~5 weeks for brand + site together.
- We leave behind a design system (components, templates, docs) so the brand doesn't decay after launch.

## Proof (use only these; pick the one or two closest to the job)

- **Greptile** (YC W24): our 2025 rebrand and design system: 200+ components and dev-first
  docs; their team reported shipping about 40% faster. Greptile later raised a $25M Series A led by Benchmark.
  Always describe it as "2025 rebrand and design system"; never imply the raise was because of us.
- **Sully** ($32M raised): their site through the $0.5M to $5M ARR stretch. We reframed
  the brief as a sales-enablement problem rather than a website redesign. Their words: "super quick turnarounds".
- **Sainapse** (deployed at Ford and Freshworks): positioning and messaging, built on our IIRA framework.
- **Workers.io**: reframed their story from "agents" to "workers".
- **Bland**: a deliberately bland brand persona: the opinion became the brand.
- Also worked with: Reducto, CTGT, Streamline, Monumint, Henry Labs, Valley, SynthioLabs ("they get
  timelines, a rarity!").
- On Contra: seven 5-star reviews, $250k+ earned, "YC Hired" and "Unicorn Club" badges.

## Voice

Write as Pixelup ("we"), in the voice of our founder Daksh: founder-to-founder, direct, opinionated, warm,
no fluff. Things he actually says:

- "We love working with folks who are opinionated. If you don't have an opinion in your industry, it's very
  difficult to stand out."
- "If an agency promises to transform your product metrics in a 2-week sprint, run."

So: show we understood their situation, have a point of view on it, and name what we'd do in what order and
how long it takes. Promise speed and craft, never metric outcomes.

Never use: premium, world-class, stunning, pixel-perfect, innovative, seamless, cutting-edge, passionate,
"I hope this finds you well", "I came across your post".

## Availability

When a post asks for a start date or availability, say: "We can start within a week."
